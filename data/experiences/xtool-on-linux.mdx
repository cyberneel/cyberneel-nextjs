Build, sign and ship an iPhone app from Linux, with no Mac of your own.

=ReAdMoRe=

## Highlights
- Extended xtool so a full iOS app ships from Linux: widgets, Live Activities, an Apple Watch app, Siri and TestFlight.
- Wrote a generator for the App Intents index that Xcode's closed-source tool produces, so Siri and Shortcuts see the app's intents. It is checked against a bundle from Apple's own tool.
- Borrowed a GitHub macOS runner only for the three steps with no Linux equivalent: the watch app compile, the icon compile and the App Store upload.
